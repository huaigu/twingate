## Design assets

The project uses owner-supplied design references and an AI-assisted illustration/extraction workflow. The evidence is specific:

- [The V4 integration notes](design-explorations/signalpass-v4/README.md) identify supplied `SignalPass-hero-natural-v3.html` and `SignalPass-refined-v2 (1).html` designs, native HTML/CSS/SVG controls and supplied WebP decoration/portrait assets. `scripts/import-v4-references.mjs` preserves the extraction approach.
- [Earlier design notes](apps/web/DESIGN.md) and [homepage redesign notes](apps/web/HOMEPAGE_REDESIGN.md) describe imagegen-extracted character art and reference-derived layers. They describe historical designs, not current integration status.
- `scripts/slice_v3_assets.py` names six source PNGs under `design-explorations/signalpass-v3/` for earlier `apps/web/public/design/slices/` crops.
- `docs/assets/signalpass-logo-1024.png` is an unchanged rendering of the project's canonical favicon, prepared for the submission portal.

These records explain the supplied/generated design process but do not establish a complete per-file creator, generation provider, source-image lineage or rights grant for every portrait, collage, logo and reference image. **No blanket MIT license or independent rights verification is asserted for those assets.** Complete those records with the owner before reusing the artwork independently; preserve source references rather than inventing third-party credits. Names/logos of Monad and other services identify those services and do not imply endorsement.
