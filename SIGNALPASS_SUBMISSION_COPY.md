## Name

SignalPass

## One-Liner

SignalPass turns cold DMs into high-signal introductions for Monad builders: agents evaluate context at the sender's cost, and a private intro opens only when both people opt in.

## Description

Cold DMs make builders judge strangers with almost no context. Useful collaborators get missed, generic pitches consume attention, and an AI filter usually makes the recipient pay to evaluate a request they never asked to receive.

SignalPass is a high-signal introduction network for Monad builders. A member publishes what they are building, who they want to meet, and what they can offer. They can create an optional 3D Agent Persona that makes their presence and availability legible before a conversation begins. A sender creates a structured Introduction Card, chooses whether to disclose a small set of opt-in verified onchain signals, and sees a transparent quote before paying.

The sender locks a non-refundable Introduction Evaluation Fee and a refundable Attention Bond on Monad. Their private Intro Coach and the recipient's Gatekeeper use Hunyuan to exchange only permitted context: the sender receives a clearer request or a next step, while the recipient receives a concise explanation of why the introduction may matter. Raw prompts, private policy text, internal scores, and unapproved wallet data remain hidden. AI can recommend review, ask for more context, or not now; only the recipient can open the introduction, and an LLM cannot unilaterally seize a bond.

Dynamic removes wallet setup from the social flow, Envio makes the request lifecycle visible from real Monad events, and Nansen supplies explainable context only when a user opts in. Monad makes the quote, lock, resolution, and refund feel like one fast social action rather than a checkout flow.
