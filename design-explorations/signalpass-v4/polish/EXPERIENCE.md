# People and correspondence: core-screen redesign

Homepage direction update, 2026-09-28: the user preferred the original animated people-and-context composition to the numbered example panels. Restore that signature on the homepage, retain concise action copy and integrated navigation, and keep the fluid discovery/composer layouts. Do not substitute generic numbered explainer cards for this animated signature in later polish passes.

Intent: a builder should quickly understand whom they can meet, why an introduction is worth considering, and what they are approving.

Hierarchy: one main action per view. People and their approved context lead discovery; readable writing leads composition; a clearly labeled sample introduction explains the homepage promise.

Palette: warm paper and ivory for reading, dark ink for information, violet for a chosen action, sage for a confirmed acceptance. Keep the supplied people; do not generate replacement UI as a bitmap.

Depth: quiet paper surfaces, thin tonal edges, restrained shadows on overlays. Avoid large illustration panels competing with controls.

Typography: Georgia provides the correspondence voice; a system sans-serif supports reading and controls. Main titles 40–64px, body 15–17px, labels 12–14px. Important controls retain readable labels and generous hit areas.

Spacing: 4px base; 24–32px reading surfaces; 16–24px related groups; 40–64px between sections. Discovery and composition use fluid layout instead of the scaled 1648px canvas.

Implementation order: shared account/navigation shell and control sizes; homepage example and message; discovery and letter composition; live/demo, wallet, motion and responsive validation.

The SuperToken image skill is available if a new bitmap materially helps. Existing avatars and paper assets are sufficient for the initial composition. Hunyuan remains unconfigured; sample people stay explicitly labeled, and no presentation code advances real delivery or payment state.
